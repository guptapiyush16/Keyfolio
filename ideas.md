# Keyfolio design brief

## Direction
**Terminal Editorial** — a playful, tactile portfolio for Piyush Gupta, inspired by the supplied reference: warm paper, ink-black outlines, yellow/coral signals, pill controls, and a developer-community personality.

The visual system stays bold and slightly hand-drawn, but the content now centers Piyush’s actual profile: backend engineering, Generative AI, applied ML, Python/FastAPI, production automation, and curiosity beyond the terminal.

- **Design movement:** editorial utility with a maker-built feel.
- **Core principles:** clarity first, proof through metrics, personality second; oversized type; visible structure; friendly navigation.
- **Color philosophy:** cream canvas as paper, ink for structure, yellow for active energy, coral for calls to action, and muted sage for secondary notes.
- **Layout paradigm:** wide centered canvas, asymmetrical hero grid, outlined cards, a proof-oriented experience timeline, and stacked anchor sections.
- **Signature elements:** terminal logo mark, yellow cursor, chunky underlines, code-style labels, keyboard keycaps, and offset printed shadows.
- **Interaction philosophy:** direct and tactile; hover lifts cards and nudges arrows; active nav pill becomes dark; reduced motion is respected.
- **Typography system:** Space Mono for labels and metadata; system sans for readable body; oversized display headings with tight tracking.
- **Brand essence:** an engineer who makes advanced AI useful in production.
- **Brand voice:** precise, warm, curious, lightly witty, and evidence-led.
- **Wordmark/logo:** `>_` terminal key inside a coral rounded square, paired with `keyfolio`.
- **Signature brand color:** coral `#ff6b4a`.

## Content pillars
The site should foreground Piyush’s measurable work: 1,000+ parallel test scenarios across 20+ microservices, 60% faster test execution, 70% lower manual QA effort, ControlNet/Stable Diffusion research at Samsung Prism, the AI-Native API Orchestration Gateway, EEG-based seizure detection, and imagined speech decoding.

## Human layer
The “Behind the keyboard” section reflects Piyush’s photography and creative expression, global volunteering experience in Vietnam, and current curiosity about LLM agents, semantic search, and robust APIs.

## Public delivery
Single-page static site with semantic section anchors, meaningful initial HTML, accessible links, responsive layout, real contact links, and `/manus-routes.json` for the current route manifest.
